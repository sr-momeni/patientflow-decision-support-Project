### 💡 Filming Tips:
*   **Hide Bookmarks:** Press `Ctrl+Shift+B` (Windows) or `Cmd+Shift+B` (Mac) to hide your browser bookmarks bar so the screen looks cleaner.
*   **Zoom Level:** Keep your browser zoom at exactly `100%` so the dashboard grid doesn't squash or stretch weirdly.
*   **Pacing:** Make sure you leave your mouse still for 2-3 seconds after clicking a tab so the viewer's eyes can adjust to the new page.
